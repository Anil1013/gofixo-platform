Gofixo Pricing

Clear pricing for home-service bookings. The final amount depends on the service and work required.

Home services

Home-service visit charge

₹250

The ₹250 visit charge applies to every home-service booking, even if the requested work is not ultimately completed. It is separate from the cost of parts, materials, labour or additional work.

  • Parts and materials: charged separately at the applicable price, when required.
  • Labour or additional work: depends on the job and will be explained to the customer before the work begins.
  • Taxes: any applicable taxes will be disclosed where charged.
Customer approval: the provider should explain applicable labour, parts and additional charges before starting chargeable work. Customers can ask for a breakdown before approving.

Ride services

For rides, the fare shown in the app before booking confirmation is the applicable quoted fare for that booking. Please review the route, distance and fare in the app before confirming. Do not rely on the home-service visit charge for rides.

Payment and refunds

Available payment methods and any booking-specific charges are shown in the app. For cancellation and refund terms, read our Cancellation & Refund Policy.
