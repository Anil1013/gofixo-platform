Cancellation & Refund Policy

This policy applies to ride and home-service bookings made through Gofixo. Booking-specific cancellation terms or charges shown in the app at the time of booking take precedence where applicable.

Cancellation

If you need to cancel, use the cancellation option in the app as soon as possible. Whether a cancellation charge applies may depend on the booking status and any terms shown before confirmation. If no cancellation charge or rule is shown, contact Gofixo support before assuming a charge applies.

Home-service visit charge

The ₹250 visit charge applies to every home-service booking, even if the requested work is not ultimately completed. Please review this charge before confirming a booking. This visit charge is separate from any parts, materials, labour, or additional work charges.

Refund requests

If you believe you were charged incorrectly, a booking was not fulfilled, or a payment was duplicated, submit a refund request through the support/contact details provided in the Gofixo app or official website. Include the booking reference and payment reference, but never share passwords, OTPs, or full card details.

Approved refunds, where applicable, are returned through the original payment method or the relevant payment provider's process. The time for the credit to appear can depend on the payment provider and bank. Submitting a request does not itself guarantee that a refund will be approved.
