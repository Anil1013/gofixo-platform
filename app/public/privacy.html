Privacy Policy

This page explains the types of information Gofixo may process when you use the website or app.

Information we may collect

  • Account details such as name and mobile number.
  • Booking information, selected services, pickup/destination or service address, and booking status.
  • Location information needed for nearby providers, navigation, distance or ride estimates, when you grant device permission.
  • Payment status and transaction references from supported payment flows. Payment credentials should be handled by the payment provider rather than stored directly by Gofixo unless explicitly disclosed.
  • For service partners, profile photographs and documents submitted for identity or eligibility checks.

How information is used

Information may be used to create and secure accounts, match bookings with providers, provide location-based features, support payments, prevent fraud, handle customer support, and meet applicable legal obligations.

Sharing

Information may be shared with the relevant customer or provider to fulfil a booking, and with service providers such as hosting, maps, communications, or payment processors when needed to operate the platform. Information may also be disclosed where legally required.

Location and security

You can manage location permissions in your device settings. We use reasonable measures to protect information, but no online system can guarantee absolute security.

Your choices

You may stop using the service and request help regarding your account information through the support/contact details provided in the Gofixo app or official website. Some records may need to be retained for legal, security, payment, or dispute-resolution reasons.
